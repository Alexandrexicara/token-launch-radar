Methodology & risk controls

A missing ticker or market identifier is only a discovery signal, not proof that a protocol has no token. Every candidate requires independent checks against official docs and token markets.

  • Wrapped, staked and receipt-like products are excluded.
  • FDV ranges are broad TVL-multiple scenarios, not forecasts.
  • Farming plans describe legitimate product usage only. No reward or airdrop is implied.
  • Never sign unknown messages, share seed phrases or approve unlimited token allowances.
